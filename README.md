# FHE Accelerator Simulators 04 — Optical NTT Engines: Precision, Conversion and Power

Fourier transforms in optics, and what it takes to map an exact modular NTT onto an analogue complex FFT: digit decomposition, small-prime RNS, rounding and error correction, ENOB, DAC/ADC energy from a Walden figure of merit, and laser and thermal-tuning power. The simulator then shows when an optical engine wins and when it loses.

Topics: Fourier optics, Digit planes, Bluestein, ENOB, Walden FoM, Laser power.
